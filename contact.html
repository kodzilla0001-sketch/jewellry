Common Questions

You Asked, We Answer

How long does a bespoke commission take?
Standard bespoke pieces take 6–8 weeks from approved design to delivery. Complex pieces or those requiring rare gemstones may take 10–14 weeks. Rush orders can sometimes be accommodated — contact us to discuss.
Do you ship nationwide across Nigeria?
Yes! We deliver to all major cities — Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Kano, Enugu and more. Orders above ₦50,000 receive free delivery. We use insured courier services for all jewellery.
Can I see pieces in person before buying?
Absolutely. Our Lagos atelier is open by appointment, Monday through Saturday. Book a private viewing via WhatsApp and we'll arrange a personal consultation. We also offer video calls for remote clients.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept direct bank transfer, Paystack, Flutterwave, debit/credit cards, and cash for in-person visits. For bespoke commissions, we require a 50% deposit at design approval, with the balance before delivery.
Do you offer ring resizing and repairs?
Yes — and for all Sparkles pieces, resizing is complimentary for the lifetime of the piece. We also offer full cleaning, polishing, prong re-tipping, and stone replacement services.
Can I use my own stone in a bespoke piece?
Absolutely — and we love this. Family heirlooms, inherited gems, sentimental stones — our goldsmiths treat them with the greatest care. Contact us to begin this special conversation.
