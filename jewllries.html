Our Philosophy

Born of Fire,
Finished with Soul

At Sparkles, ring-making is not a production line — it is a ritual. Our master goldsmiths draw from decades of tradition, shaping 18k gold by hand using techniques passed through generations, while embracing modern precision where it serves the art.

Each ring passes through at least twelve hands before it reaches yours. From the wax carver to the stone setter, every craftsperson brings their full attention to a single piece — because we believe luxury lives in the details you can feel but cannot always see.

01
Design
Sketched by hand, refined digitally into a precise wax model.
02

Lost-wax casting in 18k gold — a 5,000-year-old technique.
03
Setting
Stones placed and secured by hand under magnification.
04
Polishing
Mirror finish achieved through hours of hand-polishing.

From Idea to Icon

The Five Stages of a Sparkles Ring

Concept
Client brief, mood boards, and initial hand sketches by our lead designer.
Wax Model
A precise wax carving built by hand — the blueprint of the final piece.
Casting
Lost-wax casting in 18k gold at over 1,000°C — pure alchemy.
Stone Setting
Diamonds and gemstones placed under magnification by our setters.
Quality & Polish
Final inspection, certification, and hand-polishing to a mirror finish.
