How It Works

Five Steps to Something Eternal

Consultation
A private conversation — in person, by phone, or via WhatsApp — where we listen to your vision, your story, your desires.
Design Sketch
Our designers produce hand-drawn sketches and digital renderings until the design feels unmistakably yours.
Wax Prototype
A physical wax model is carved and presented for your approval before a single gram of gold is touched.
Goldsmithing
Lost-wax casting in 18k gold, followed by stone setting, engraving, and meticulous hand-finishing over 8–12 hours.
Delivery & Legacy
Presented in our signature box with a certificate of authenticity. This piece is yours — and the story behind it, ours to keep.
