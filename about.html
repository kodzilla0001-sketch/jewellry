Our Story

"Jewellery is not mere decoration — it is memory, identity, and love made tangible."
Founder, Sparkles Jewelleries

Sparkles was founded with a singular vision: to bring world-class handcrafted jewellery to Nigerian women who deserve nothing less than the extraordinary. What began as a small atelier in Lagos has grown into Nigeria's most trusted name in luxury adornment.

Every piece we create is a conversation between heritage and modernity — drawing from Africa's rich aesthetic traditions while embracing contemporary design. We don't just make jewellery. We craft heirlooms.

Our Journey

A Decade of Excellence

From a single workshop to Nigeria's premier jewellery destination — every year has deepened our craft.

2015
The Beginning
Founded in Lagos with a vision to make luxury accessible to the African woman. Our first atelier opens with just five signature pieces.
2017
Diamond Certification
We become one of the first Nigerian jewellers to exclusively use GIA-certified diamonds — setting a new standard in the industry.
2020
Bespoke Studio
Launch of our dedicated bespoke design studio, offering fully custom pieces from concept to creation — a first in Lagos.
2025
The Lumière Era
Our most ambitious collection yet — 300+ pieces blending African artistry with contemporary luxury for the modern Nigerian woman.

From Concept to Wrist

How We Craft

Every Sparkles piece follows a meticulous five-stage process that ensures perfection in every detail.

1
Design
Our designers sketch and refine concepts — balancing artistic vision with wearability and the desires of the woman it's made for.
2
Wax Carving
Master carvers sculpt the design in wax — a tactile process that brings the sketch into three dimensions with extraordinary detail.
3
Casting
The wax model is cast in 18k gold using the lost-wax method — an ancient technique perfected over thousands of years.
4
Stone Setting
Our stone setters place each certified diamond or gemstone by hand, securing it for a lifetime of wear without compromise.
5
Polish & Quality
The finished piece is polished to a mirror sheen, inspected under magnification, and certified before it reaches your hands.
